About FlowerCrafts

At FlowerCrafts we handcraft beautiful, long-lasting bouquets for every occasion. Each piece is designed and assembled by local artisans using high-quality materials and attention to detail.

Our Mission

We believe flowers (and the craft of making them) bring joy. Our mission is to create unique, eco-conscious arrangements that brighten homes and celebrations while supporting local makers.

What makes us different

  • Handmade designs — no mass-produced arrangements.
  • Curated materials and sustainable packaging.
  • Personalized service — add a custom message at checkout.

Shipping & Returns

We ship nationwide. Orders are dispatched within 2 business days. Because items are handmade, returns are accepted within 7 days for defective products — please contact us first.
